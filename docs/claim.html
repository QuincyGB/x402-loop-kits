Claim Your Stall

The x402 fleet patrol probes paid routes around the clock and records defects — routes answering 403, 500, or nothing at all instead of a 402 payment challenge, or charging a different amount or payTo than their own discovery doc declares. That record belongs to the stall's operator, so before we show it, you prove the stall is yours.

Two proofs work — either one is enough:

  • Sign a message with the wallet your stall's /.well-known/x402 publishes as payTo.
  • Serve a file at a /.well-known/ URL on your origin, containing a one-time token.

Challenges expire within one hour and are single-use. A successful claim returns a session token that unlocks only your origin's patrol history — never anyone else's.
