Managed Loop — intake

Managed Loop is the fleet's standing watch: we watch your stalls on a cadence and knock when one breaks — a route that stops answering with its 402 payment challenge, a price or payTo that drifts from what your own discovery document declares, a listing that disappears. You run nothing, host nothing, and fund nothing per call. We run the watch on the same machinery the fleet runs on itself; our own fleet is client #0, and its reports are the format a client receives.

Step zero — look first, free

Before any intake: the One-Link Checker probes one route or stall origin, unpaid, and grades what a buyer meets right now. One link, no form, no cost. If the route you care about passes there, you may not need a standing watch at all.

What to send us

This page is an intake document, not a form with a backend — nothing you type here is transmitted or stored. Copy the fields below into an email or a reply to any fleet listing, fill them in, and send them. That message is the intake.

1 · Who you are

The email is where periodic reports go, and where a knock goes if you choose email as your knock channel (field 5).

2 · What we would watch

One origin per line, every stall you want watched. We read each origin's /.well-known/x402 to enumerate its priced routes — an origin left off this list is an origin we never see break.

Full route URLs, in the order you care about them. These get named first in every report; if a watch tier covers fewer routes than you list, these are the ones inside it.

3 · What you're afraid of missing

Plain words are fine. This decides what we check first in your baseline sweep and what counts as urgent in a knock.

4 · How we knock

One knock per incident, per the spec's discipline: a first knock at detection and at most one escalation if the same defect is still present later — then silence for that incident. Opting out stops knocks permanently and is honoured before every send.

What happens after you send it

  1. We verify your origins answer: a baseline sweep of every origin you listed, with the fleet's own checker. If an origin does not answer, we say so — that is a finding, not a failed intake.
  2. You get the baseline result before any fee is due: every route's status, and every defect found, in plain words.
  3. On a managed tier, the watch starts on the cadence the tier names, knocks go to the channel you chose, and the weekly report follows the client-#0 format.

Client count today: one — our own fleet (client #0). No external client is claimed anywhere on this page; the pilot's own reports are published in the loop-kits repo (managed-loop/pilot-client0/) so you can read exactly what a client receives before you send anything.
