Index Subscriptions — the catalog watch, as a subscription

StallRow's Verdict Index snapshots the full x402 Bazaar catalog about every 6 hours and diffs it snapshot-to-snapshot. An index subscription turns that diff into a feed for your stall: register a filter once, and every delivered evaluation pushes the full diff for your listings — adds, removals, price changes, payTo rotations — to your webhook. Empty diffs arrive as heartbeats, so silence never masquerades as health.

How it works

StepCallPrice
RegisterPOST /v1/index/subscribe with a filter (pay_to, origin, or service), a webhook URL, and a Fleet Pass id for fundingfree
BaselineFirst evaluation stores your starting snapshotfree — never debited
EvaluatePOST /v1/index/subscription/run — diffs the newest snapshot against your watermark and pushes it to your webhook $0.10 debited from your Fleet Pass per delivered evaluation (the /v1/index/changes price)
StatusPOST /v1/index/subscription/statusfree

Funding honesty is built in: if your pass can't cover an evaluation, the run reports funding_failed and your watermark does not advance — you never lose a diff to a dry pass, and a failed delivery on our side refunds the debit. If our own snapshot pipeline is stale, evaluations are skipped, not faked (see the live example below).

Live dogfood: our own stalls, on our own subscription

Verdict Row eats its own cooking. On 2026-10-27 we registered our own fleet on the subscription — filter pay_to = the fleet payTo, funded by our own $5 Fleet Pass:

POST /v1/index/subscribe
→ {"subscription_id": "isub_0bae95e3ed874ed4", "status": "active", ...}

POST /v1/index/subscription/run        (first evaluation)
→ {"status": "baseline", "snapshot_id": 3,
    "current_at": "2026-10-27T11:39:12Z", "debited_units": 0}

Two hours later the next evaluation did something more instructive than deliver: it refused. The snapshot-ingest run behind the Index had failed its own settlement-signature reconciliation check, so the newest snapshot was stale — and the subscription declined to diff against stale data rather than sell us a confident-looking feed built on it:

POST /v1/index/subscription/run        (second evaluation)
→ {"status": "skipped",
    "reason": "latest index snapshot predates the newest ingest run",
    "debited_units": 0}

That is the product working as designed: a skipped evaluation costs nothing, advances nothing, and says why. When the ingest pipeline is healthy again, the next run delivers the full diff since the baseline — nothing was lost by waiting.

What a delivered evaluation looks like

A delivered run returns (and webhooks) the complete diff for your filter since your last delivered evaluation: listings added and removed, and field changes with before/after values — the same diff the fleet's public drift board renders for the whole catalog, narrowed to your stall.
