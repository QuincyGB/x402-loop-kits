One-Link Checker

Paste one x402 route URL — or a stall origin — and see what a buyer meets right now: an unpaid probe asks the route for its 402 payment challenge and grades what actually comes back. The same probe and the same defect classes as a paid Fleet Check sweep, for one link, free.

How it works, stated plainly: the probe never pays, signs, or settles anything. It sends one unpaid request, reads the HTTP status and — on a 402 — whether the challenge parses as x402 v2 with accepts, and whether the live amount and payTo match what the origin's own /.well-known/x402 declares. Rate limit: 10 probes per minute per address. Private and loopback targets are refused.

Batch check — up to 10 links at once

Paste up to 10 route URLs or stall origins, one per line, and get one combined report — the same probe, the same defect classes, every row graded separately. Batch limit: 3 batches per minute per address.
